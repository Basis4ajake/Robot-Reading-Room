The Andes mountain range runs along the western edge of South America and is the longest continental mountain range in the world.

The Nile river flows through northeastern Africa and is traditionally considered the longest river on Earth.

Mount Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa, rising to about 5,895 meters above sea level.

The Amazon rainforest spans nine countries in South America and produces roughly twenty percent of the world's oxygen, though this figure is debated by scientists.

The Sahara desert is the largest hot desert in the world, covering much of North Africa.

The Great Barrier Reef off the coast of Australia is the largest coral reef system on Earth, visible even from outer space.

Lake Baikal in Siberia is the deepest freshwater lake in the world, holding roughly a fifth of the planet's unfrozen fresh water.
